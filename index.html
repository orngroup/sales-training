Objection Handling — Part 1

Turning "No" Into "Yes"

💰
"Your price is too high."
Don't defend — differentiate. Ask what competitor included. Show what they'd lose at a lower price. Reframe around value, not cost.
📅
"Those dates don't work."
Offer alternatives immediately — don't ask, suggest. Have ±2 weeks checked before the call. Give a reason to love the new dates.
🤔
"Let me think about it."
Smoke out the real objection. "Is it the price, the dates, or the itinerary?" Solve the actual concern — not the excuse.
👨‍👩‍👧
"I need to ask my family."
Invite them in. Offer a visual summary or hotel photo pack they can share. Make the decision easy for the whole family.
🏨
"That hotel has bad reviews."
Acknowledge — then offer control. Swap the hotel at the same price, or flag their preferences to the property directly.
✈️
"I found flights cheaper online."
Let them — then show what they lose. Package protection means if a flight cancels, we re-book everything. Solo bookers are on their own.
Objection Handling — Part 2

More Objections, More Answers

🌧️
"Won't it be rainy / monsoon?"
Turn weather knowledge into confidence. Thailand's regions have different seasons — when Phuket is wet, Koh Samui is dry. We plan around the weather. (See destination wiki for month detail.)
🍜
"My kids are fussy eaters."
Reassure with specifics. Indian restaurants in every tourist hub. Thai food — rice, noodles, grilled dishes — kids love. We flag dietary notes to hotels in advance.
🛡️
"Is Thailand safe?"
Separate perception from reality. Millions of Indian families visit every year. Tourist areas in Bangkok, Phuket, and Chiang Mai are extremely well-set-up. We include 24/7 emergency support.
🛂
"Is the visa complicated?"
Make it effortless. Indian passports get visa-on-arrival. We handle all documentation guidance, arrival card, and fees. You just bring passport photos.
📱
"I can plan this on Google."
Agree — then show what they can't Google. Supplier relationships, negotiated rates, and what happens at 11pm in a Bangkok hotel when something goes wrong.
🔄
"What if we need to cancel?"
Lead with policy, close with reassurance. Changes allowed up to [X] days. Travel protection add-on covers medical/family cancellations. Peace of mind for a small cost.
Key Tactics

Price, Dates & Urgency

📐
Break Price Per Day
₹1,20,000 for 2 people sounds big. ₹8,500 per person per day — for luxury hotels and private transfers — sounds like a deal.
"Less than a nice dinner out. For a week in Thailand."
🔀
Trim, Don't Slash
Never cut the price outright. Offer to downgrade one element — or keep everything and add a freebie. Give them a choice, not a discount.
"Would you prefer we save ₹12K on hotels, or keep it and add a sunset cruise?"
📊
Always Show 3 Options
Good / Better / Best. The premium tier makes mid look reasonable. Customers who see one price argue. Customers who see three pick the middle.
"The middle option converts at the highest rate, every time."
📅
Link Dates to Experience
Don't apologise for alternative dates — make them the better option. Off-peak = less crowds, lower prices, clearer water. Make them feel they discovered something.
"October is actually the best-kept secret for Thailand."
⏰
Waitlist for Sold-Out Dates
Put them on priority waitlist. 1-2 cancellations per departure is normal. Hold their name while they confirm budget. Creates urgency without pressure.
"I'll hold your name — we typically get cancellations."
⚡
Real Urgency Only
Never manufacture urgency. But real urgency exists every day — school holidays, visa timelines, airline seats, hotel allocations. Know your product. Tell the truth.
"Customers who trust you act faster. Always."
Know Your Customer

B2B vs B2C — Different Games

🤝
Sanjay Travels
Trade Agent · B2B
They care about
Margin. Reliability. Speed. They don't want the romance of Thailand — they want to look good to their client and earn their commission.
Your language
"Your margin on this is ₹X per person." · "I can hold the price for 48 hours while you close your client." · "Want a co-branded itinerary?"
Never do
Contact their client directly · Undercut them on a direct enquiry · Take more than 4 hours to respond
"Make them look like a hero to their customer."
👨‍👩‍👧
Mr & Mrs Garg
Direct Customer · B2C
They care about
Safety. Value for money. The feeling of being looked after. They don't buy itineraries — they buy confidence that everything will go smoothly.
Your language
"This is what I'd book for my own family." · "I've been to Phuket — let me tell you which area to stay in." · "I'm your single point of contact, even after you land."
Never do
Send a generic PDF without a personal note · Disappear after taking the deposit · Use travel jargon they don't understand
"Make them feel like your only customer."
Role Play — Scenario 1 · B2B

Sanjay Travels · Thailand · Price Objection

🤝
Sanjay Travels
Trade Agent · 2 Adults + 1 Child · 7 nights BKK + Phuket
SANJAY
"Your quote is ₹1,85,000. Competitor is ₹1,60,000. My client is asking why."
AGENT
"What's in their quote? Same hotels, same transfers? Our price includes private airport transfers and your margin of ₹12,000. Let me send two versions — A at ₹1,85K full experience, B trimmed to ₹1,62K. Both keep your ₹10K+. Ready in 30 mins?"
SANJAY
"OK but client says can you do ₹1,55,000?"
AGENT
"At ₹1,55K I'd need to move to 3-star hotels and shared transfers — that changes the experience significantly. Your client came to you for quality. I can do ₹1,65K and I'll add a Phi Phi Islands day trip at no charge. That's an upgrade at a lower price — stronger sell for you."
What the agent did right
Asked first — didn't concede immediately on price
Protected Sanjay's margin — made him feel looked after
Offered choice, not a cut — gave Sanjay a tool to close his client
Set a deadline (30 mins) — kept momentum
Added value instead of dropping price — day trip upgrade
🎭 TRAINER PUSH-BACKS
"Can you do Phuket only, no Bangkok?" and "Client wants to book in cash, is there a discount?" Trainee must handle both without dropping margin below ₹8K.
Role Play — Scenario 2 · B2C

Mr & Mrs Garg · Family Thailand · 3 Objections

👨‍👩‍👧
Mr Garg
Direct · 2 Adults + 2 Kids (7 & 10 yrs) · 8 nights
MR GARG
"We want Thailand in May. Budget around ₹2.5 lakh. But I'll need to show my wife the hotels."
AGENT
"₹2.5 lakh for 4 pax, 8 nights is very doable — I'll build something lovely. I'll send a visual pack with hotel photos you can share with Mrs Garg tonight. May is peak season so hotels fill fast — if she likes it, I can hold everything with just ₹10,000 for 72 hours. Shall I have it ready in 2 hours?"
MR GARG
"Yes. But May 20-28 might not work — daughter's school exams."
AGENT
"Actually May 28 onwards is even better — post-holiday rush is over, slightly lower prices, less crowded beaches. I'll include June 1–9 options in the same pack. You'll have two great choices to show your wife."
MR GARG
"I saw Thailand for ₹80,000 on MakeMyTrip."
AGENT
"Those packages exist — they're usually shared transfers, budget hotels, and no support on the ground. Our package includes private transfers, handpicked family-friendly hotels, kids meal planning, and me on WhatsApp the entire trip. The difference shows when something goes wrong at 11pm."
Every objection handled
Budget concern — validated as achievable, pivoted to building value
Need to ask wife — turned into a tool, introduced urgency + next step
Dates problem — reframed alternative as the better option, no apology
MMT cheaper — differentiated on inclusions + peace of mind, didn't criticise
🎭 TRAINER PUSH-BACKS
"My friend went and said the food wasn't good for Indian families." and "Can we add a third adult — my mother-in-law is coming." Handle both smoothly without restarting the quote process.
